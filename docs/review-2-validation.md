## Recommended Review 2 test task

1. Sign in.
2. Open AI Assist.
3. Enter a real or controlled campus-waste description.
4. Review the AI category, summary and missing-field suggestions.
5. Correct anything inaccurate.
6. Continue to the report form.
7. Complete before evidence.
8. Submit the report.
9. Have Authority review and assign it.
10. Have Staff update the task and add after evidence.
11. Have Authority verify the result.

## Evidence to retain

Use filenames that make the session traceable, for example:

- review2_t01_ai_assist.png
